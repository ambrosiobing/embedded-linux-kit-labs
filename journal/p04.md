# P04: a build that had never passed, hidden by how it was reported

The lab is [STWIN.box vibration and ultrasound USB gateway](../chapters/04-stwin-box-vibration-and-ultrasound-usb-gateway.md),
and it is the only lab whose software exists, so it is the only one that could
have had this problem.

## What was wrong, and for how long

Beside P04's ordinary build sits a stricter one. The `strict` target rebuilds the
same sources with `-Wconversion`, `-Wsign-conversion` and `-Wdouble-promotion`
added on top of an existing `-Werror`.

**It had exited 2 on every run the repository had ever had.**

Not one of those runs reported a failure. Every one showed a full column of green
steps.

## Why nobody saw it

The step is marked `continue-on-error` deliberately and is named "reported but
not gating". That choice was sound and is unchanged.

What was not foreseen is how such a step is reported. **A failing
`continue-on-error` step is recorded as success.** Its failure survives only as
an annotation on the run, which appears as a number in a summary line. The runs
said "3 annotations" and that number was read as noise three times across
**Sunday 4 October 2026** and **Monday 5 October 2026** before anyone opened it.

The deduction of which step it was came from the anchor on the failure
annotation, `#step:11`, and step 11 was the strict pass. That was confirmation
rather than inference, which matters in a volume that has had to withdraw
inferences.

## What it was hiding, Tuesday 6 October 2026

Four findings, all of one kind, on two adjacent lines of the test file:

```
tests/test_p04.c:202  implicit conversion from float to double
tests/test_p04.c:203  to match other operand of binary expression
```

Both lines compute a magnitude as `sqrt((double)re[8] * re[8] + ...)`. Only the
first operand of each multiply carries its cast, so the second is promoted
implicitly to match it. Casting both fixes all four and changes no arithmetic
whatsoever: the products were always computed in double, and the source now says
so rather than leaving it to the promotion rules.

**Nothing in `src/` was flagged at all.** The library code was already clean
under the two warnings that matter most for this lab, whose whole discipline is
samples staying `int16_t` until the one place they become floats. The fault was
in the test's own convenience arithmetic, which is the least alarming place it
could have been.

## What changed, and what did not

The step is still non-gating. **What failed here was a report nobody opened, not
a missing gate**, and converting it into a gate would have hidden the lesson
rather than taught it.

What changed is that the volume now records the shape of the blindness as well as
the fix, and that annotations get opened rather than counted.

## The thing to carry elsewhere

A check that can pass while failing is worse than no check, because it produces
the appearance of coverage. This one did it honestly, by design, and still cost
three runs of blindness. Any arrangement where a failure is recorded as a success
deserves the same suspicion, whatever the reason for it.
